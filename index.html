              A FRIENDLY REMINDER

              The shape of a hand

              Each player gets two private cards. Make the best five-card poker hand from those and up to five shared cards on the felt.

              Pre-flopBlinds are posted, then the first round of betting.

              FlopThree shared cards arrive after a burn card.

              Turn & riverOne card each, with a burn before each deal.

              ShowdownBest hand wins. Side pots and ties are shared fairly.

              Raise amounts are shown as a total for this betting round. This table runs offline on one device. Online multiplayer is not supported.
